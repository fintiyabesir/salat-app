Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Awqat does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no Awqat server.

Location

If you allow it, Awqat reads your device's location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. That calculation happens entirely on your device, and your location is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can instead choose a city by hand, and you can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings.

Settings and notifications

Your preferences — such as calculation method, chosen city and reminder settings — are stored only on your device, and shared with Awqat's own widgets and watch app on your devices so they can show the same times. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally by your device; they are not sent from a server.

Official timetables

For some regions Awqat can compare its calculation with a timetable published by a religious authority. Where this is enabled, your device downloads the published timetable directly from that authority's public website. No location or personal information is included in that request, and Awqat never receives it.

Tracking and third parties

Awqat contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Awqat is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect in the app.
