Support

Help with Awqat on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS.

Contact

Email besirunlu@hotmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub. Please include your device, your city and the calculation method you use.

My prayer times differ from my local mosque

Open Settings and choose the calculation method and Asr juristic setting used in your area. Mosques sometimes add a few minutes of precaution; Awqat shows the astronomical time for the method you choose.

Times are for the wrong place

Allow location access, or choose your city by hand in Settings. Awqat works fully offline once a location is set.

I am not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Awqat in your device settings, and that each prayer's reminder is turned on in Awqat's notification settings.

Qibla direction looks wrong

Move away from metal objects and electronics, and calibrate your compass by moving the device in a figure-eight.
